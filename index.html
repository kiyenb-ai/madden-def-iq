Test notes

Voice input uses browser speech recognition where available, and may require HTTPS or an online recognition service depending on the browser. No audio recordings are saved by this prototype. Recognition uses Tesseract.js loaded from a public CDN, so internet access is required for OCR. Images are processed in your browser by the recognition library; this prototype does not upload or save video to our servers. Browser camera permission and a secure origin are required. When any field is missing, automatic recommendations are withheld to avoid stale plays. The recommendation rules are football-concept examples, not verified optimal Madden 27 play selections. Voice uses your browser's speech feature when supported.
